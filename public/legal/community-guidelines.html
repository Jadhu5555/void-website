Community Guidelines

Last updated: 15 November 2026

VOID is an 18+ space. Help keep conversations respectful, consensual and safe.

Respect people

  • No threats, harassment, bullying, hate or targeted abuse.
  • Respect boundaries. Stop contacting someone if they ask you to stop.
  • Do not impersonate another person or misrepresent yourself to harm someone.

Protect privacy

  • Do not publish another person's personal information without permission.
  • Do not pressure anyone to reveal their identity, location or contact details.
  • Do not share private images or recordings without the subject's consent.

Keep it lawful

  • No sexual content involving minors, exploitation or non-consensual intimate content.
  • No illegal goods, threats, scams, malware or instructions intended to cause harm.
  • No content that violates applicable law or another person's rights.

Remember the 60-second design

Messages are intended to expire after 60 seconds, but recipients may capture or copy content before it disappears. Share carefully.

Enforcement

Violations may result in content removal, restrictions or account/session access being suspended where supported by the service. If VOID provides an in-app reporting feature, use it to flag harmful conduct. Do not use the service to retaliate.

Questions about this page? Contact the VOID team through the official VOID service.
