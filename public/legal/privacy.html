Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This page describes the privacy approach currently stated for VOID. VOID registration does not ask users for an email address, phone number or real name. A user can enter with a temporary username. Users may also choose to create an account; the current product information supplied to us says no personal registration information is collected and only a temporary username is used.

Information you provide

For temporary entry, you provide a username for the session. Do not use your real name or include personal details in your username or messages.

Messages and images

Messages are designed to be automatically deleted after 60 seconds. Images are described as temporary. The exact technical retention period for images, backups, delivery queues and service logs has not yet been independently confirmed. Please do not treat temporary messaging as a guarantee that content cannot be captured by another user.

Technical information and service providers

Even when an app does not ask for personal registration details, its hosting, networking, security and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, connection details, device/browser information, timestamps or diagnostic logs. The precise information processed by VOID's current infrastructure and its retention periods still need to be verified. This policy does not claim that such technical data is never processed.

How to protect yourself

  • Do not share passwords, financial details, addresses or other sensitive information in chat.
  • Do not assume a disappearing message cannot be screenshotted or copied.
  • Use a username that does not identify you.

Children

VOID is intended only for people aged 18 or older. People under 18 must not use the service.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the product and its infrastructure are verified and changed. The date at the top indicates the latest published revision.

Important implementation note

Before treating this page as a final legal policy, VOID's operator should verify actual Appwrite/Cloudflare configuration, access logs, analytics, image storage, backups, message deletion jobs and third-party processing. Update this page if actual practices differ.
