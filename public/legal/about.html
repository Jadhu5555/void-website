About VOID

Last updated: 15 November 2026

VOID is an online chat space for adults aged 18 and above. It offers two ways to enter: use a temporary username for an anonymous session, or create an account.

Our idea

We want joining a conversation to feel simple. VOID is designed around temporary identities, conversations that are intended to disappear after 60 seconds, and temporary image sharing.

What VOID offers

  • Anonymous entry with a temporary username.
  • An option to create an account.
  • Online chat and an Android app.
  • Messages designed to expire automatically after 60 seconds.
  • Temporary image sharing.

Our approach

We encourage respectful conversations and thoughtful sharing. Temporary messages are not a guarantee that recipients cannot capture, copy or otherwise retain content before it disappears.
