Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What is VOID?

VOID is an 18+ online chat service with anonymous temporary-username entry and an option to create an account.

Do I need to register?

No. You can enter with a temporary username. Account creation is optional.

What information do I need to provide?

The current product description says temporary entry uses a temporary username and does not ask for personal registration details such as email or phone number. Technical service providers may still process connection or diagnostic information.

Do messages stay forever?

Messages are designed to automatically disappear after 60 seconds.

Can I send images?

Image sharing is temporary. The exact image retention and deletion behavior should be checked in the live app before relying on a particular expiry time.

Can I use VOID if I am under 18?

No. VOID is for adults aged 18 and above only.

Are disappearing messages completely private?

No online message can be guaranteed impossible to copy or capture. A recipient may take a screenshot or another recording before content disappears.

Where can I use VOID?

You can open the web experience or download the Android app from the VOID homepage when the APK download is available.
